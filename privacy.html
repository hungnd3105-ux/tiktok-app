Privacy Policy

App: HUNG ND Content Tool
Effective date: October 19, 2026

HUNG ND Content Tool ("we", "the App") is a content management tool that helps creators schedule and publish their own videos to TikTok. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

When you connect your TikTok account through Login Kit, with your permission we receive:

  • Your TikTok Open ID and basic profile information (display name, avatar).
  • An access token that lets the App act on your behalf within the scopes you approve.
  • Videos, captions and settings that you choose to upload through the App.

We do not collect your TikTok password.

2. How we use information

  • To publish or upload videos to your TikTok account when you request it.
  • To show your connected account and the status of your posts.
  • To keep the App secure and working properly.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Sharing

Your content is sent only to TikTok, through TikTok's official APIs, to complete the actions you request. We do not share your information with other third parties unless required by law.

4. Storage and retention

Access tokens and uploaded files are stored securely and kept only as long as needed to provide the service. Uploaded video files are deleted after they are posted to TikTok.

5. Your choices

You can disconnect the App at any time in your TikTok settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services). You can also email us to request deletion of all data related to your account; we will process it within 30 days.

6. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be posted on this page.
